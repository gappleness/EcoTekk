Before you replace anything

Three questions worth two minutes.

Is it the device, or what plugs into it?

Cables, chargers and adapters fail far more often than the thing they power.

Can someone nearby fix it?

A repair is often cheaper than a replacement, and keeps a working device out of the waste stream.

Could someone else still use it?

Wipe it, reset it, pass it on. Recycling is for what can’t be reused.

My old phone has a swollen battery. Can I just drop it in a mall bin?
Please stop charging it and don’t squeeze or puncture it. Swollen batteries are a fire risk, so ask the drop-off first whether they take damaged batteries, and call ahead before you go.
